Retail

Waman Hari Pethe Jewellers

A landmark jewellery brand with a legacy spanning over a century, Waman Hari Pethe Jewellers demanded a cooling solution that matched the premium, heritage ambiance of their showroom. The challenge: deliver powerful cooling with zero visual intrusion — no exposed piping, no bulky grilles, no aesthetic compromise in a space designed to showcase India's finest jewellery.

  • Precision cooling load calculation for high-occupancy jewellery showroom
  • Concealed ductable AC system with custom diffusers to match interior design
  • Silent cassette units in display areas for customer comfort
  • Dedicated cooling zones for showcases, customer seating and billing areas
  • Full copper piping concealed within false ceiling and wall chases
  • Commissioning, handover and post-installation 6-month support contract

Commercial

Clancy Global

Clancy Global, an international professional services firm, required a full HVAC system for their Mumbai office — one that could support a dynamic work environment with open plan areas, conference rooms, server infrastructure and executive spaces, all with individual temperature control and energy efficiency as key priorities.

  • Full HVAC system design including VRV multi-zone indoor system
  • Separate controlled zones for conference rooms, server room and common areas
  • Server room dedicated precision AC with N+1 redundancy setup
  • Fresh air intake and exhaust ventilation for occupied zones
  • Smart thermostat and BMS interface for centralised control
  • Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (CAMC) post-installation
  • Quarterly preventive maintenance with performance reporting

Healthcare

Cloudnine Hospital

Healthcare HVAC is in a league of its own. Cloudnine Hospital engaged Cool Air Enterprises for multi-location hospital-grade climate and ventilation systems — installations where infection control compliance, precise temperature zoning and absolute reliability are non-negotiable. This was our most technically demanding engagement and one we executed without a single snag.

  • Hospital-grade HVAC design per NABH and ASHRAE 170 guidelines
  • Precision temperature and humidity control for OTs, ICUs and NICU
  • HEPA-filtered fresh air systems for infection-sensitive zones
  • Positive pressure in OTs and negative pressure in isolation wards
  • Separate air handling for maternity, paediatric and general wards
  • Energy-efficient chiller system across multi-floor hospital building
  • 24/7 monitoring integration and emergency response protocol
  • Ongoing CAMC with dedicated service engineer allocation
