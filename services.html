  • Concealed copper piping and trunking

Service 03

AMC / CAMC Contracts

Keep your investment protected and your comfort guaranteed with our flexible maintenance contracts. We offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for labour coverage and Comprehensive AMC (CAMC) covering both labour and spare parts — giving you complete peace of mind.

  • Refrigerant level check and top-up (CAMC)

VENTILATION
Service 04

Ventilation & Fresh Air Systems

Healthy indoor air quality goes beyond cooling — it requires proper ventilation, fresh air exchange and humidity control. We design and install mechanical ventilation systems that remove stale air, control pollutants and maintain optimal CO₂ levels for occupant wellbeing.

  • Fresh Air Handling Units (FAHU / ERV)
  • Exhaust ventilation for kitchens, bathrooms and car parks
  • Positive pressure ventilation for server rooms
  • HEPA and activated carbon filtration systems
  • Kitchen exhaust hood and grease duct installation
  • Stairwell and basement pressurisation systems
  • Smoke extraction and fire-rated duct installations
